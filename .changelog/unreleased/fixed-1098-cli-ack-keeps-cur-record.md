- **The CLI's mail ack keeps an acknowledged `cur/` record, stamped, as the openclaw-tps-mail ack already does.** This covers `tps mail ack`, the agent runtimes' ack and the channel bridge's. On the local route, a reply obligation's receipt scan reads the delivered reply in the recipient's maildir, so a reply the recipient checked and acked before the obligation settled now settles it; in 0.10.0 the ack deleted that record, and the obligation ended `failed` with a nack to the sender. The kept record is removed by the existing mail gc (`tps mail check`, `tps mail gc`). An ack of a record still in `new/` or `dlq/` deletes it without stamping it first; if the delete fails, the ack now fails with the path and the error instead of reporting success. On restart, the channel bridge no longer re-verifies or re-claims a record it already forwarded or acked.
