- **Relay reconnect claims require an absent record, verified ownership, or a recorded process that is gone or has a mismatched start token.** The first claim of a connection attempts the write and writes the record only while the stop gate is open. A later claim writes when the record is absent, is verified as owned by this connection, or belongs to a connection whose recorded process is not running or is running with a start token different from the recorded one (pid reuse). A record whose token was altered is indistinguishable from pid reuse. An invalid, unreadable or unverifiable record is left as it is; the claim reporter emits one diagnostic line for an ownership or readability refusal, and a later refresh may report separately.
