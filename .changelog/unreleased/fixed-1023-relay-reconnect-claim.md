- **Guard relay claims after the first attempt with record ownership and process liveness.** On the first claim attempt, the connection attempts a write without an ownership check, only while the stop gate is open; it can replace an invalid file. A claim after that attempt checks eligibility only while the stop gate is open, and attempts the write when the record is absent, is verified as owned by this connection, or belongs to a connection whose recorded process is not running or carries a different `proc:` token (pid reuse). A live `ps:` mismatch is unverifiable because a record written before cli#1045, or by an older process, may hold local-time text. On claims after the first attempt, an invalid, unreadable or unverifiable record is left as it is; the claim reporter emits one diagnostic line for an ownership or readability refusal, and a later refresh may report separately.
