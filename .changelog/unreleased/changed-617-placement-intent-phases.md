- **Mail placement intents carry a phase; MailClient recovery, the CLI lease sweep and promotion act only on `placing` (Refs #617, slice a).** An intent without a `phase` is `placing` and is handled exactly as before; no writer emits another phase yet. On those paths, a record whose intent declares `in_progress`, or a phase or schema version the reader does not understand, is never finished, replaced, presented or checked out. Such an intent is not quarantined solely for an unfamiliar phase, version or liveness field, and it keeps only its own file's delivery gate closed. An intent that declares `failed` keeps no gate closed and is not presented on those paths. It stays until an operator removes it, and its payload follows ordinary `cur/` archive and GC retention. Once that payload has left `cur/`, a different message may reuse the name: the marker moves to `.placement-<hash>.failed-<messageId>.json`, which keeps its consumed ID retained.

  The bridge and OpenClaw plugin startup sweeps call `recoverPromoted` directly and do not read phases yet; slice (b) covers them.

  A placement intent whose `file` is not a plain basename (empty, `.`, `..`, or containing `/`, `\` or NUL) is now refused on write and quarantined on read.
