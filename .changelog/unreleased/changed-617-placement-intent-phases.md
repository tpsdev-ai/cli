- **Mail placement intents carry a phase; MailClient recovery, the CLI lease sweep and promotion skip or preserve non-`placing` intents (Refs #617, slice a).** An intent without a `phase` is `placing`. An otherwise-valid older intent whose `file` is a plain basename is handled exactly as before, records with no intent are handled as before, and no writer emits another phase yet. On those paths, a record whose intent declares `in_progress`, or a phase or schema version the reader does not understand, is never finished, replaced, presented or checked out. Such an intent is not quarantined solely for an unfamiliar phase, version or liveness field, and it keeps only its own file's delivery gate closed. An intent that declares `failed` leaves its per-file (filename) gate open, while its message ID stays refused by the consumed-ID (replay) gate; it is not presented on those paths. It stays until an operator removes it, and its payload follows ordinary `cur/` archive and GC retention. One exception to preserving a non-`placing` intent: once a `failed` payload has left `cur/`, a different message may reuse the name, and its placement moves the marker to `.placement-<hash>.failed-<messageId>.json`, which keeps the old consumed ID retained.

  The bridge and OpenClaw plugin startup sweeps call `recoverPromoted` directly and do not read phases yet; slice (b) covers them.

  A placement intent whose `file` is not a plain basename (empty, `.`, `..`, or containing `/`, `\` or NUL) is now refused on write and quarantined on read. That includes an older intent the previous parser accepted when its hashed name matched: the one exception to legacy parity.
