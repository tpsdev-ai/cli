- **Mail placement intents carry a phase, and every reader leaves alone a phase it does not finish (Refs #617, slice a).** An intent without a `phase` is `placing` and is handled exactly as before; no writer emits another phase yet. MailClient recovery, the CLI lease sweep and `promote` never finish, replace, present or check out a record whose intent is `in_progress` or in a phase or schema version they do not understand, and such an intent is never quarantined as malformed: it keeps only its own file's delivery gate closed. A terminal `failed` intent keeps no gate closed and is never presented; the intent file stays until an operator removes it, and its payload follows ordinary `cur/` archive and GC retention.

  A placement intent whose `file` is not a plain basename (empty, `.`, `..`, or containing `/`, `\` or NUL) is now refused on write and quarantined on read.
