- **Branch pid records are claimed and removed under one inter-process lock (Closes #730).** `tps branch init` and `tps branch start` read, classify, spawn and write the record while holding it; `tps branch stop`, daemon shutdown and stale-record replacement use the same lock. A launcher or stop that cannot acquire the lock within its timeout refuses, naming the lock path. A record replaced between a stop's read and its unlink is left in place.
