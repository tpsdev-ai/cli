- **MailClient recovers verified interrupted placements with matching identity (Closes #600).** Recovery verifies a `cur/` copy and, under the mailbox lock, matches its recorded identity and pre-verification raw bytes before finishing the intent and returning the message. Verifier unavailability or a lock failure defers recovery; a `placing` record that verifies as neither supported format, or as both, is quarantined after the identity and byte checks. A present mismatched copy or an older-build intent without identity whose only copy is in `cur/` is withheld with its intent pending.
