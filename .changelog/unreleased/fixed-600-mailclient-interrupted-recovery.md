- **MailClient can recover interrupted first deliveries (Closes #600).** Recovery verifies a `cur/` copy and, under the mailbox lock, matches its recorded device/inode and pre-verification raw bytes before finishing the intent and returning the message. Verification or lock failure defers recovery. A present mismatched copy or an older-build intent without identity whose only copy is in `cur/` is withheld with its intent pending.
