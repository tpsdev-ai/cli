- **A mailbox record update refuses a record directory that does not resolve to the locked mailbox at check time.** Under the lock, `updateExistingRecord` compares the physical identity (device and inode) of the lock root with that of the record directory's real parent and refuses with `MailRecordLockRootError` when they differ, so an update through a symlinked `cur/` does not write another mailbox under a different lock. A party that does not hold this lock can still swap the directory for a symlink between the check and the write; that window predates this change and is out of scope.
