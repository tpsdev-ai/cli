- **A relay disconnect whose `close()` rejects is reported and retried by `stop()`.** A rejected close retains the channel; if `stop()`'s retry also rejects, the host record is kept and `stop()` throws `HostChannelCloseError`. Today's WS and TCP channels resolve `close()` on timeout, so this branch is reached through the channel interface (see #774). Connect-time host-record write failures are reported before reconnecting.
