- **Breaking: relay and bridge stops refuse invalid or unverifiable pid records.** `tps mail relay stop` and `tps bridge stop` keep these records and exit non-zero. An unchanged stale record can be removed. After stopping a bridge process, the same stop can also remove an unchanged duplicate record for that process. [#990](https://github.com/tpsdev-ai/cli/pull/990), [#1086](https://github.com/tpsdev-ai/cli/pull/1086).
