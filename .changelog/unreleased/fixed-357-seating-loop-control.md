- **Office supervisor**: `shutdown_children` sends the stop signal, polls until the whole-second `SECONDS` deadline (at least the grace, at most the grace plus one second and one poll), stopping early when all tracked PIDs exit, attempts KILL for every tracked PID still alive, clears the tracked list and only then removes the PID file. A signal that arrives between a launch and the tracking of its PID is recorded by the handler and acted on right after the PID is tracked. `SHUTDOWN_GRACE_SECONDS` and `TPS_SUPERVISOR_TEST_SIGNAL_AFTER_LAUNCH` are checked at startup and again after the secrets are loaded. The trap control accepts the literal signal traps and the pinned EXIT trap, and rejects a literal `eval` token. The seating-loop control covers explicit `exit`/`return` forms, a self-kill (`kill $$` or `kill -SIG $$`) and calls to supervisor functions that exit, and it refuses a loop containing a multi-line quoted string or heredoc.
