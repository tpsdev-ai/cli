- **Office supervisor**: `shutdown_children` sends the stop signal, waits out a ~10 s TERM grace (counted sleeps), attempts KILL for every tracked PID still alive, clears the tracked list and only then removes the PID file. A signal that arrives between a launch and the tracking of its PID is recorded by the handler and acted on right after the PID is tracked. The trap control accepts the literal signal traps and the pinned EXIT trap, and rejects `eval`. The seating-loop control covers explicit `exit`/`return` forms, a self-kill (`kill $$` or `kill -SIG $$`) and calls to supervisor functions that exit, and it refuses a loop containing a multi-line quoted string or heredoc.
