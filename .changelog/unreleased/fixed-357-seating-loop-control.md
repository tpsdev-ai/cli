- **Office supervisor**: `shutdown_children` sends the stop signal, waits out a ~10 s TERM grace (counted sleeps), attempts KILL for every tracked PID still alive, clears the tracked list and only then removes the PID file, so a second call signals nothing. A signal that arrives between a launch and the tracking of its PID is recorded by the handler and acted on right after the PID is tracked. The control requires exactly one EXIT/0/SIGEXIT trap, with the pinned action `shutdown_children TERM`: a second trap, a reset, an arbitrary-PID kill or a rewritten action fails. The seating-loop control covers explicit `exit`/`return` forms, a self-kill (`kill $$` or `kill -SIG $$`) and calls to supervisor functions that exit, and it refuses a loop containing a multi-line quoted string or heredoc.
