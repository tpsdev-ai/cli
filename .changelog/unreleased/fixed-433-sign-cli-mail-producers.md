- **Every CLI-internal mail producer signs its body through the one signing path `tps mail send` uses (Refs #433).** Pulse notifications, topic fan-out and catch-up, hire onboarding, roster invites, bootstrap's introduction and health probe, branch handler replies and rewritten forwards, and `@tpsdev-ai/agent`'s `MailClient.sendMail` previously wrote a body straight into a maildir, which a `promote()`-reading recipient dead-letters terminal. Each now signs as its sender — or, for the system-origin producers, as the CLI's own identity; a producer with no key refuses with the named error and writes nothing. The openclaw-tps-mail plugin's outbound adapter and the channel bridge are unchanged (slice B).
