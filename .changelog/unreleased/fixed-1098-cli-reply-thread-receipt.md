- **A local reply sent with `tps mail send --reply-to` settles its obligation even after the recipient acks it.** The send also writes a receipt for the reply into the sending agent's own obligation store, when that agent has a mailbox under the mail directory; receipts older than seven days are removed on the next such send. The openclaw-tps-mail receipt scan accepts the receipt only when the signed reply it holds verifies as the obligated agent's reply to the inbound's sender on that thread. In 0.10.0, such a reply acked by the recipient before the obligation settled left no evidence, and the obligation ended `failed` with a nack to the sender.
