- **A reply sent with `tps mail send --reply-to` settles its obligation even after the recipient acks it.** A local send from an agent that has a mailbox under the mail directory also writes a receipt for the reply into that agent's own obligation store; receipts older than seven days are removed on the next such send. The openclaw-tps-mail receipt scan accepts the receipt only when the signed reply it holds verifies as the obligated agent's reply to the inbound's sender on that thread. In 0.10.0, a reply the recipient acked before the obligation settled left no evidence, and the obligation ended `failed` with a nack to the sender.
